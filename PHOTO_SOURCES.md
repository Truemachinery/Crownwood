# Photo sources

The public site uses the following non-generated media:

- `public/images/pct/*.webp`: extracted from the corresponding manufacturer product sheets linked at [PCT Fastphalt Road Building Products](https://www.procoat.tech/asphalt-road-building-products).
- `public/images/field/crownwood-road-application.webp`: still from Crownwood's `public/Videos/IMG_0705.MOV` field-application video.
- `public/Videos/permabase-black-application.mp4`: web playback copy of Crownwood's `public/Videos/IMG_0705.MOV`. All 1,190 source video frames are retained in their original order (approximately 39.68 seconds), with no scene edits or speed changes. The original portrait orientation is preserved at 720 × 1280. Encoded as H.264 High / yuv420p with AAC stereo audio and MP4 fast-start metadata; the iPhone HLG / BT.2020 footage is converted to SDR / BT.709 for browser display. The file is 8,352,346 bytes, down from the 52,642,449-byte original. Camera location and other capture metadata are omitted from this delivery copy.
- `public/images/field/permabase-black-application.webp`: full-frame still at 00:32 of the same Crownwood source video, showing the truck applying material and the wet road surface. Extracted with the same HLG-to-SDR color conversion and encoded as a 1080 × 1920 WebP (411,174 bytes). No objects, textures, or road conditions were generated or retouched. This is application footage, not a before-and-after comparison or a photograph of a fully cured surface.
- `public/images/field/concrete.webp`: [Anil Sharma / Pexels](https://www.pexels.com/photo/construction-worker-pouring-concrete-on-site-34842259/), free to use under the Pexels license.
- `public/images/field/land-clearing.webp`: [Alfo Medeiros / Pexels](https://www.pexels.com/photo/excavator-in-a-forest-27036801/), free to use under the Pexels license.
